Google indexes that and serves it keyless, with no account. This is where SSO/auth infrastructure gets found — wikipedia.org names auth.wikimedia.org, twitter.com still names x.com — neither is reliably discoverable by scanning for subdomains.

GHunt is best known for its email module: paste an address, get the linked Google account's profile, Play Games, Maps reviews and Calendar. That is deliberately absent — it authenticates as a Google account using a session cookie, which this platform does not ingest. What runs here is the keyless half. Paste a DOMAIN, not an email address.
